i Welcome to the FSGP 2027 sim for Halo — Georgia Tech Solar Racing’s car.

Practice on Ozark International Raceway to learn the circuit and feel the car. You can also tweak experimental tunable car parameters (mass, motors, grip, and more) while you explore.

Race is cockpit-style: put down clean laps and climb the boards. Chase Pace (fastest lap) or Efficiency (lowest energy) — each has its own leaderboard!

Per FSGP rules: efficiency runs need an average over 35 mph, and you can’t go 65 mph or faster on a valid lap. Stay on track and have fun.
